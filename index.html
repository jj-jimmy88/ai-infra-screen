How this ranking works

Every company is scored on ten measurable factors — no opinions, no manual ratings. Each factor is computed from public market and financial data, weighted, and combined into one score from 0–100%. The ten factors and their plain-English meaning are shown inside every stock's detail view.

Factors marked NA (data not published for that company) are excluded from both sides of the calculation, so thin-coverage companies aren't unfairly punished. Rows scored on incomplete data are flagged PARTIAL DATA; fully-scored rows are flagged None.

Companies facing active fraud litigation are excluded from scoring (VETO). ETFs are shown for reference (ETF) but not scored on company factors. You can add any US ticker with the box at the top — it appears immediately and is fully scored on the next weekly refresh once added to the screening universe.

This is a screening tool, not investment advice. A high score means a company currently fits these ten criteria — it is not a prediction or recommendation.
