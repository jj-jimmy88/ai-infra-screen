How this ranking works

Every company is scored on ten measurable factors — no opinions, no manual ratings. Each factor is computed from public market and financial data, weighted, and combined into one score from 0–100%. The ten factors and their plain-English meaning are shown inside every stock's detail view.

Factors marked NA (data not published for that company) are excluded from both sides of the calculation, so thin-coverage companies aren't unfairly punished — but their "data weight" is shown, and scores built on little data deserve less trust.

Companies facing active fraud litigation are excluded from the ranking entirely rather than scored — a lawsuit isn't a minus-three, it's a different category of risk. ETFs are shown for reference but not ranked (too few factors apply).

This is a screening tool, not investment advice. A high score means a company currently fits these ten criteria — it is not a prediction, a recommendation, or a substitute for your own research. Markets can and do defy every factor here.
